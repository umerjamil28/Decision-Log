I spent most of the hour on the landing page. The brief said that matters more than the product, and its also the first thing anyone sees. I put a real entry on the page instead of a fake dashboard screenshot. If that specimen looks like the demo, the site feels like one thing.

I pushed the design away from the usual generated look. Paper, serif, hairline rules, no card grid, no badges. Copy is a bit dry on purpose. If it reads like a committee wrote a SaaS homepage I missed.

What I did not build
- no login, no database, no localStorage. they said persistence is optional and i didnt want a half-working save
- no search, tags, comments, assignees. thats a different tool
- no pdf export. i talked about reading last quarter. the demo is just the log
- no tooltip tour. four fields
- no dark mode. the paper thing only works light

Routes are normal paths (/demo). A 404.html sends GitHub Pages back to the app.

The seeded rows are the products own calls plus one inactive entry so you can see both states without typing.
